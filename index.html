Who We Are

Odisha Student Association, New Delhi is a community created for students from Odisha living in New Delhi. The association aims to bring students together and keep them connected with Odisha's culture, traditions and community life while they are away from home.

Our Purpose

Many students living in Delhi cannot participate in cultural celebrations taking place in Odisha. The association seeks to recreate the spirit of Odisha in Delhi through cultural and community programmes such as Nuakhai, Raja, Utkala Dibasa, Ganesh Puja and other traditions.
